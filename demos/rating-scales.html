How it works

Each statement shows how the room rated it from 1 (disagree) to 5 (agree) as a stacked bar, with the average beside it. Simulated ratings arrive over about three seconds. Use the 1–5 buttons to rate the highlighted statement; the panel then moves on to the next one. Reset clears your ratings.
