How it works

The chart shows 2019–2022 and hides the rest. Drag the four dashed handles, or focus one and use the up and down arrows, to draw your prediction; Reveal then draws the actual line and reports how far off the guess was on average. Committing first makes the surprise stick. Reset tries again.
