How it works

Move the request and agent sliders; utilisation, average wait and the curve update live. Utilisation ρ = requests ÷ (agents × 6), since each request takes 10 minutes. Wait ≈ 10 min × ρ^(√(2(agents+1))−1) ÷ (agents × (1−ρ)). Watch the wait explode as ρ nears 100%. Reset restores 30 requests, 6 agents.
