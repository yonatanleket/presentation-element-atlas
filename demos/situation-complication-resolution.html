Situation–Complication–Resolution

Illustrative

Why we are asking for a pilot

Situation

Three teams share one release process that worked when we had ten engineers.

→
Complication

Headcount doubled; releases now wait nine days for manual sign-off.

→
Resolution

Replace sign-off with automated checks, piloted with one team in Q3.

How it works

The opener runs in three beats. Situation states the shared context, Complication adds the tension that makes it a problem now, and Resolution answers it with the proposal. Play reveals one beat per step, with the resolution emphasized because it is the point of the deck.
